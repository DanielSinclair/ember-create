# Bay Day

A personal outing planner for Alameda Seaplane and Downtown San Francisco.
The app includes a selected weekday sailing subset from SF Bay Ferry's
[schedule effective November 2, 2026](https://d262kwcyxl71on.cloudfront.net/wp-content/uploads/20261012200345/SFBF-Seaplane-Redlined-Schedule-Nov-2-2026.pdf):

| Direction | Depart | Arrive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Alameda → San Francisco | 6:30 AM | 6:50 AM |
| Alameda → San Francisco | 7:25 AM | 7:45 AM |
| Alameda → San Francisco | 8:45 AM | 9:05 AM |
| San Francisco → Alameda | 7:50 AM | 8:10 AM |
| San Francisco → Alameda | 10:25 AM | 10:45 AM |
| San Francisco → Alameda | 11:25 AM | 11:45 AM |

These are selected published sailings, not a complete timetable or live feed.
The app links to the [official route page](https://www.sfbayferry.com/routes-schedules/alameda-seaplane/) for current service information.
